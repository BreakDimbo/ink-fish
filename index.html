水墨游鱼
ink-fish

Brush-ink fish swimming in the paper behind your page. Move the cursor slowly and the curious ones come to look; flick it and they scatter; click in open water to startle them. 鼠标慢慢移动，好奇的鱼会游过来；快速一划，它们四散；点击空白处会惊走附近的鱼。

Obstacles

These cards are passed as obstacles: carp and koi swim around them and the canvas is erased underneath.

Ink under text

The headline and intro are passed as text, so ink thins where it crosses them.

Three depths

Far minnows, mid carp, near koi — each layer has its own scroll parallax and soft lamp shadow.

Burst & glide

A body wave runs head to tail; the tail beat follows speed and thrust.

Light / dark

Follows prefers-color-scheme or <html data-theme>; try the toggle above.

Cheap

One canvas, one rAF loop, paused when nothing is visible.

Element mode · 局部水池

Mounted on this box with InkFish.mount(pond, { count: 14 }). It pauses while scrolled out of view.

Usage

<link rel="stylesheet" href="src/ink-fish.css">
<script src="src/ink-fish.js"></script>
<script>
  var fish = InkFish.mount(document.body, { obstacles: ".card", text: "h1, .intro" });
  // fish.set({ theme: "dark" });  fish.destroy();
</script>
