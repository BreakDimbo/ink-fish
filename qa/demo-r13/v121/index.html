01

Usage用法

Two files, one call. No dependencies and no build step — a plain <script> tag. Purely decorative: the canvas never takes pointer events and is hidden from screen readers.

两个文件，一次调用。零依赖、无需构建。纯装饰：不接收鼠标事件，对读屏软件隐藏。

Page background · 整页
<link rel="stylesheet" href="src/ink-fish.css">
<script src="src/ink-fish.js"></script>
<script>
  // a full-page pond behind everything
  var fish = InkFish.mount(document.body, {
    fixed: true,                 // the pond stays put; the page scrolls over it
    ripples: true, koi: true,    // rings under the cursor; a few painted koi
    obstacles: ".card",          // swum around; no ink under them
    text: "h1, .intro",          // ink thins under this text
    title: "h1"                  // the koi give the headline room
  });

  fish.set({ theme: "dark" });   // change options at runtime
  fish.destroy();                // remove the canvas and every listener
</script>
Element mode · 局部水池
// the canvas scrolls with the element and pauses while it is off screen
InkFish.mount(document.querySelector("#pond"), { count: 14, koi: 2 });
Auto-init · 自动初始化
<body data-ink-fish>
<div data-ink-fish='{"count": 20, "theme": "dark"}'></div>

04

Technique原理

  • i

    One canvas, one loop

    A fixed 1/60 s simulation step and rAF drawing, DPR capped at 1.5. Paused when the tab is hidden or no open water is visible; layout is never read inside a frame.

  • ii

    A still pond

    Far minnows, mid carp, near koi, painted far to near. With fixed the pond stays put and the page slides over it: a scroll only moves the cut-outs, never the canvas, and never triggers layout.

  • iii

    Spine and body wave

    An 11-segment spine follows the head; joints bend at most ~11°, so a tight turn reads as a curve, never a fold. A wave runs head to tail, its beat tied to speed and thrust.

  • iv

    Burst and glide

    A short burst, then a long glide; schooling within each species by separation, alignment and cohesion.

  • v

    Ink, not vectors

    Bézier bodies with ink pooled at the head and washing out toward the tail; paler fins; a dorsal brush stroke and eyes on carp and koi.

  • vi

    Shadows without blur

    A quarter-resolution canvas of its own, scaled up by the compositor, so interpolation softens them for free. Under reduced motion the fish barely drift, redrawn about ten times a second.

  • vii

    Rings on the surface

    A short train of three wavefronts per ring: a hairline of cool shade on the inner slope, a little thicker on one side, a hairline of light on the outer. Where a ring crosses a fish, its outline bends a pixel or two, radially.

  • viii

    Pigment, then ink

    Koi patches live in body space — seeded, lobed, bending with the spine and clipped where they wrap the back — each laid in as a wash with a darker wet edge, a paler heart and a little granulation.
